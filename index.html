Before You Begin

Educational use and source-verification notice
Due Diligence is a Philippine Bar review and study platform. Legal provisions, jurisprudence, and references are manually verified against primary sources where available. However, the suggested answers, assessments, scores, and AI-generated explanations presented by the platform are not official answers prescribed, issued, or endorsed by the Supreme Court of the Philippines.

Due Diligence is intended solely for legal education and Bar examination preparation. It does not provide legal advice and should not replace independent verification using official sources.
