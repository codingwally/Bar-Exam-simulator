## Root-cause themes

1. **Local evidence is mistaken for authoritative state.** Flags and grading drafts need durable server revisions while retaining local recovery journals.
2. **Supporting systems are coupled to core state.** Email, downloads, and AI must be independent jobs with explicit, non-authoritative status.
3. **Legacy operational ceremony dominates the interface.** Required Beadle, multiple keys, role walls, and internal enums make a straightforward task feel technical.
4. **Tests validate components, not lived workflows.** A passing mock or source-string assertion cannot prove a browser, file, inbox, network transition, or human decision.
5. **Recovery is implicit.** The target makes every interruption visible, preserves evidence, and gives the user a bounded next action.

## Traceability rule

Implementation work may not close a finding with a screenshot or unit test alone. Closure requires the named automated regression, the named human scenario, objective evidence, an independent reviewer, and Wally's release approval. A recurrence reopens the finding and blocks release.
