    Student privacy notice

    Before the examination begins

    This preview shows what every student must review before examination questions become available.

    Information used
    Full name, student number, subject, year level, examination answers, save and submission records, and any safeguards selected by the professor.
    Purpose
    Verify eligibility, deliver the examination, preserve answers, support grading, release results, investigate documented incidents, and meet academic-record obligations.
    Access
    The assigned professor and specifically authorized school administrators. Due Diligence personnel receive access only when required for protected support or recovery.
    Retention and rights
    The school’s published retention and appeal policy applies. Students may request access or correction and may contact the school’s privacy office.

    The school remains responsible for confirming the final notice, lawful basis, retention period, contact information, accommodations, and any recording consent with its Data Protection Officer and counsel.
