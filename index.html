Platform Disclosure

Please read this entire disclosure before continuing.

Due Diligence is a law-school learning companion and essay-practice platform. Its primary purpose is to provide students with a reliable environment in which to practice identifying issues, organizing legal reasoning, applying rules to facts, and writing clear conclusions.

Due Diligence is not a law firm, legal-advice service, law school, or official Bar authority. The platform does not provide legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client or other professional relationship. It is not a substitute for instruction from professors, assigned materials, independent legal research, or advice from a qualified lawyer.

Questions, suggested answers, citations, scores, explanations, and feedback may be prepared or assisted by one or more artificial-intelligence models. These materials are educational aids. They may be incomplete, outdated, inconsistent, or incorrect. Suggested answers are not infallible and may not represent the only legally defensible answer. AI-generated scores may differ from those given by a professor, examiner, lawyer, or court. Always verify legal rules and authorities using current official primary sources.

No score, feedback, suggested answer, or activity on Due Diligence guarantees or predicts a user’s academic grades, Bar examination performance, admission to practice, employment, or professional success.

Digital services may change, become temporarily unavailable, or fail to preserve drafts and results. Keep an independent copy of important work. Do not submit confidential, privileged, case-sensitive, or third-party personal information.

Please report inaccurate questions or answers, questionable citations, grading concerns, accessibility problems, unclear instructions, and technical issues through Support. Good-faith reports help improve the platform and are handled only as described in the current Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Before You Begin

Educational use and source-verification notice
Due Diligence is a Philippine Bar review and study platform. Legal provisions, jurisprudence, and references are manually verified against primary sources where available. However, the suggested answers, assessments, scores, and AI-generated explanations presented by the platform are not official answers prescribed, issued, or endorsed by the Supreme Court of the Philippines.

Due Diligence is intended solely for legal education and Bar examination preparation. It does not provide legal advice and should not replace independent verification using official sources.
