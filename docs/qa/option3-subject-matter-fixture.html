Practice question

What three basic elements must generally be present for a contract to exist under the Civil Code?

How to approach

List every item requested, then briefly explain each one. For this kind of question, completeness and accuracy matter more than a long factual application.

Your answer

A valid contract generally requires consent of the contracting parties, a certain object that is the subject of the contract, and a lawful cause for the obligation.

Your response shows a solid grasp of the core idea. Review the legal basis below and compare it with your answer to strengthen your understanding.

Reveal suggested legal basis
Civil Code, Article 1318 — consent of the contracting parties, an object certain which is the subject matter of the contract, and the cause of the obligation.
Guidance: why this basis applies
Article 1318 states the three essential elements of a contract: (1) consent of the parties, (2) object certain which is the matter of the contract, and (3) a cause or consideration that gives rise to the obligation. All three must be present for a contract to exist. This question asks for an enumeration of those elements, and Article 1318 provides that exact list.
